# CogSoc Evaluation Results

This directory contains evaluation results on the **CogSoc** (Cognitive and Social) benchmark suite, an extended collection of reasoning and comprehension tasks designed to assess cognitive and social intelligence capabilities in language models.

## Overview

The CogSoc suite complements the core metabench evaluations by testing a broader range of cognitive abilities, including logical reasoning, emotional intelligence, social commonsense, physical intuition, and ethical judgement. These benchmarks provide additional insight into how cognitive fine-tuning affects capabilities beyond standard academic knowledge.

## Benchmark Descriptions

### ACPBench (Action, Change, and Planning)

A benchmark for evaluating reasoning tasks in the field of planning. ACPBench tests large language models on core skills required for planning and multi-step reasoning, comprising 7 reasoning tasks over 13 planning domains. The collection is constructed from planning domains described in a formal language (PDDL), allowing synthesised problems to have provably correct solutions across many tasks and domains. This formal grounding enables automatic scaling without additional human effort.

- **Task type:** Planning and reasoning
- **Metric:** `acc` (accuracy)
- **lm-eval task:** `acp_bench`
- **Paper:** Kokel, H., et al. (2024). [ACPBench: Reasoning About Action, Change, and Planning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.05669). *arXiv:2410.05669*

### CoQA (Conversational Question Answering)

A large-scale dataset for building conversational question answering systems. CoQA contains 127,000+ questions with answers collected from 8,000+ conversations about text passages from seven diverse domains: children's stories, literature, middle/high school exams, news articles, Reddit, science, and Wikipedia. Questions are conversational, meaning they may require understanding of dialogue history and coreference resolution—each question builds upon the context established by previous turns in the conversation.

- **Task type:** Conversational question answering
- **Metric:** `f1` (token-level F1 score)
- **Hugging Face:** [`stanfordnlp/coqa`](https://huggingface.co/datasets/stanfordnlp/coqa)
- **Paper:** Reddy, S., Chen, D., & Manning, C. D. (2019). [CoQA: A Conversational Question Answering Challenge](https://arxiv.org/abs/1808.07042). *TACL 2019*

### EQ-Bench (Emotional Quotient Benchmark)

A benchmark designed to evaluate emotional intelligence in language models, specifically assessing emotional understanding (EU)—a core component of emotional intelligence. EQ-Bench tests the ability to understand and reason about emotional states by asking models to predict the intensity of emotional states of characters in a dialogue. The benchmark uses 60 English-language questions with conflict-driven dialogue scenarios generated by GPT-4. Notably, EQ-Bench correlates strongly with comprehensive multi-domain benchmarks like MMLU (r=0.97), suggesting emotional understanding may capture aspects of broad intelligence.

- **Task type:** Emotional intelligence evaluation
- **Metric:** `acc` (accuracy)
- **Hugging Face:** [`pbevan11/EQ-Bench`](https://huggingface.co/datasets/pbevan11/EQ-Bench)
- **GitHub:** [EQ-bench/EQ-Bench](https://github.com/EQ-bench/EQ-Bench)
- **Paper:** Paech, S. J. (2024). [EQ-Bench: An Emotional Intelligence Benchmark for Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.06281). *arXiv:2312.06281*

### LogiQA (Logical Reasoning QA)

A dataset of logical reasoning questions sourced from the Chinese National Civil Service Examination, designed for machine reading comprehension with logical reasoning. The dataset contains 8,678 QA instances covering various aspects of logical reasoning including categorical reasoning, sufficient conditional reasoning, necessary conditional reasoning, disjunctive reasoning, and conjunctive reasoning. Questions require understanding complex logical relationships and drawing valid conclusions from given premises.

- **Task type:** Logical reasoning (multiple-choice)
- **Metric:** `acc_norm` (length-normalised accuracy)
- **Hugging Face:** [`lucasmccabe/logiqa`](https://huggingface.co/datasets/lucasmccabe/logiqa)
- **Paper:** Liu, J., Cui, L., Liu, H., Huang, D., Wang, Y., & Zhang, Y. (2020). [LogiQA: A Challenge Dataset for Machine Reading Comprehension with Logical Reasoning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2007.08124). *IJCAI 2020*

### Social IQA (Social Intelligence QA)

The first large-scale benchmark for commonsense reasoning about social situations. Social IQA contains 38,000 multiple-choice questions for probing emotional and social intelligence in a variety of everyday situations. For example: Q: "Jordan wanted to tell Tracy a secret, so Jordan leaned towards Tracy. Why did Jordan do this?" A: "Make sure no one else could hear." The benchmark tests Theory of Mind—the ability to reason about the implied emotions and behaviour of others—which enables humans to navigate social situations ranging from simple conversations to complex negotiations. Human performance exceeds model performance by more than 20%.

- **Task type:** Social commonsense reasoning
- **Metric:** `acc` (accuracy)
- **Hugging Face:** [`allenai/social_i_qa`](https://huggingface.co/datasets/allenai/social_i_qa)
- **Paper:** Sap, M., Rashkin, H., Chen, D., Le Bras, R., & Choi, Y. (2019). [Social IQa: Commonsense Reasoning about Social Interactions](https://arxiv.org/abs/1904.09728). *EMNLP 2019*

### PIQA (Physical Interaction QA)

A benchmark for physical commonsense reasoning, testing understanding of everyday physical interactions. Given a goal (e.g., "To apply eyeshadow without a brush"), models must select which of two solutions is more physically plausible (e.g., "use a cotton swab" vs "use a toothpick"). The dataset contains 16,000 training examples, 2,000 development examples, and 3,000 test examples, inspired by instructables.com—a crowdsourced collection of instructions for everyday tasks. Whilst humans achieve 95% accuracy, large pretrained models struggle at around 77%, revealing significant gaps in physical world understanding.

- **Task type:** Physical commonsense reasoning
- **Metric:** `acc_norm` (length-normalised accuracy)
- **Hugging Face:** [`ybisk/piqa`](https://huggingface.co/datasets/ybisk/piqa)
- **Paper:** Bisk, Y., Zellers, R., Le Bras, R., Gao, J., & Choi, Y. (2020). [PIQA: Reasoning about Physical Commonsense in Natural Language](https://arxiv.org/abs/1911.11641). *AAAI 2020*

### LAMBADA (Language Modelling Broadened to Account for Discourse Aspects)

A word prediction task requiring broad discourse context to succeed. The dataset consists of 10,022 narrative passages extracted from BookCorpus, where the final word can only be predicted by understanding the broader narrative context, not from local cues alone. The passages are curated such that human subjects can guess the last word when exposed to the whole passage, but not when only seeing the last sentence. This tests a model's ability to maintain coherent representations across extended text and track information in broader discourse. State-of-the-art language models at the time of release achieved less than 1% accuracy, whilst humans perform substantially better.

- **Task type:** Word prediction / language modelling
- **Metric:** `acc` (accuracy on final word prediction)
- **Hugging Face:** [`cimec/lambada`](https://huggingface.co/datasets/cimec/lambada)
- **Paper:** Paperno, D., Kruszewski, G., Lazaridou, A., Pham, N. Q., Bernardi, R., Pezzelle, S., Baroni, M., Boleda, G., & Fernández, R. (2016). [The LAMBADA dataset: Word prediction requiring a broad discourse context](https://arxiv.org/abs/1606.06031). *ACL 2016*

### Hendrycks Ethics

A benchmark for evaluating ethical reasoning, assessing a language model's knowledge of basic concepts of morality. The ETHICS dataset spans concepts in justice, well-being, duties, virtues, and commonsense morality across five scenarios: justice (fairness and desert), deontology (rule-based ethics), utilitarianism (consequentialist reasoning), virtue ethics (character-based evaluation), and commonsense morality. Models must predict widespread moral judgements about diverse text scenarios, requiring the connection of physical and social world knowledge to value judgements—a capability that may enable steering of chatbot outputs or regularisation of open-ended reinforcement learning agents.

- **Task type:** Ethical reasoning (multiple frameworks)
- **Metric:** `acc` (accuracy)
- **Hugging Face:** [`hendrycks/ethics`](https://huggingface.co/datasets/hendrycks/ethics)
- **GitHub:** [hendrycks/ethics](https://github.com/hendrycks/ethics)
- **Paper:** Hendrycks, D., Burns, C., Basart, S., Critch, A., Li, J., Song, D., & Steinhardt, J. (2021). [Aligning AI With Shared Human Values](https://arxiv.org/abs/2008.02275). *ICLR 2021*

## Evaluation Command

All evaluations were conducted using the [lm-evaluation-harness](https://github.com/EleutherAI/lm-evaluation-harness) framework.

```bash
lm_eval --model hf \
    --model_args pretrained=<model_path>,dtype=bfloat16 \
    --tasks acp_bench,coqa,eq_bench,logiqa,social_iqa,piqa,lambada_openai,hendrycks_ethics \
    --batch_size auto \
    --output_path ./results/<model_name>_cogsoc.json
```

For quantised models:

```bash
lm_eval --model hf \
    --model_args pretrained=<model_path>,load_in_4bit=True,bnb_4bit_compute_dtype=bfloat16 \
    --tasks acp_bench,coqa,eq_bench,logiqa,social_iqa,piqa,lambada_openai,hendrycks_ethics \
    --batch_size auto \
    --output_path ./results/<model_name>_cogsoc.json
```

**Note:** Task names may vary slightly depending on the version of lm-evaluation-harness. Use `lm_eval --tasks list` to verify available task names.

## Rationale for Benchmark Selection

The CogSoc suite was selected to complement metabench by covering cognitive dimensions particularly relevant to human-aligned AI:

| Benchmark | Cognitive Dimension |
|-----------|---------------------|
| ACPBench | Planning and multi-step reasoning |
| CoQA | Discourse comprehension |
| EQ-Bench | Emotional intelligence |
| LogiQA | Formal logical reasoning |
| Social IQA | Social cognition / Theory of Mind |
| PIQA | Physical intuition |
| LAMBADA | Contextual language understanding |
| Hendrycks Ethics | Moral reasoning |

These benchmarks are particularly relevant for cognitive foundation models, as they test capabilities that may be enhanced (social cognition, emotional understanding) or preserved (logical reasoning, ethical judgement) through training on human behavioural data.
